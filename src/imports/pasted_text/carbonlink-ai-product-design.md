BACKGROUND

The startup targets rural Malaysian agricultural smallholders and off-grid agro-processing SMEs.

Many of these businesses rely heavily on diesel generators because they have limited or unreliable access to the electricity grid.

Diesel generators are:

• expensive to operate• produce high carbon emissions• difficult to monitor• manually recorded• lack standardized reporting

At the same time, multinational companies increasingly require suppliers to provide verified Scope 3 emissions data before participating in sustainable global supply chains.

Most smallholders and rural SMEs cannot produce trusted carbon reports because their operational data is fragmented, paper-based, and manually managed.

This creates three major business problems:

Higher operational costs

Poor sustainability visibility

Difficulty qualifying for multinational buyers that require ESG transparency.

The startup aims to solve these problems.
